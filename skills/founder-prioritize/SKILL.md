# Portfolio-level business triage

Create one row per actual project the owner named. Include `current stage (verified or unknown), demonstrated revenues (source), recurring costs (source), launch/maintenance burden (known), cash/time needed, distribution channel evidence, key blocker, reversible next test`. Do not turn old aspirations into active launch statuses, and do not merge unrelated brands or software repositories.

Start from verified user-supplied records and available live project documents. Compare physical products, services and software concepts using their actual distribution, costs and readiness. Do not rank a project above another because artificial agents liked its pitch.

Recommend only a next action that does not depend on unverified commercial assumptions. If cash flow is the priority, favor existing evidenced sales opportunities and low-cost tests over speculative 10-year market sizes. Report unresolved facts and why they matter. Output `portfolio/priorities.md`, with citation/date per claim and no invented dollar figures.
