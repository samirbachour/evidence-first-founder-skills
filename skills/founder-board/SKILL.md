# Concept review: three independent lenses

Read actual `founder/idea.md`, user notes, and any cited evidence before evaluating. If no idea exists, create a draft only from facts the owner supplied; use `UNKNOWN` for unstated facts. Keep separate files per business.

1. **Offer economics lens**: Inspired by Alex Hormozi's *$100M Offers* (2021). Evaluate urgency of the buyer's problem, purchasing ability, channel access, specific outcome, credible proof, effort, and guarantee delivery cost. This book is one lens, not proof of demand. Current author bibliography also includes *$100M Leads* and *$100M Money Models*; see `research/RESEARCH_NOTES.md`.
2. **Defensibility and distribution lens**: Adapted from Peter Thiel and Blake Masters' *Zero to One* (2014). Evaluate small initial market, differentiation, costs to reach buyers, copyability, team, timing, and defensibility. A local service or apparel store can succeed without tech-company monopoly characteristics; this lens must not automatically reject it.
3. **Product experience lens**: Based on published descriptions of Steve Jobs's product philosophy in Walter Isaacson's *Steve Jobs* (2011). Evaluate buyer journey from first exposure to delivery, quality, friction, accessibility, and where to simplify. Do not simulate Jobs's voice or claim endorsement.

Write `founder/board.md` with each lens's `supported strengths / concerns / missing evidence / conditional recommendation`. Numeric scores are optional but never evidence. State disagreements. Write next *verification* action, not a fictional investor vote. If market demand, inventory, purchase data or economics are absent, mark the verdict **unverified**, and route to `founder-validation` or `founder-cfo`.
