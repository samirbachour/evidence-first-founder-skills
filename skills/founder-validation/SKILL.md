# Evidence audit and real customer validation

This skill is the gatekeeper for all founder decisions. Do not populate missing facts from model knowledge or plausible examples. For every consequential claim, track `claim, source URL or uploaded file, exact data, observed date, retrieval date, classification, limitations, verification owner`.

**Evidence classes:** (1) owner-provided exports/invoices/order transactions and first-party documents; (2) live dated public sources; (3) explicit numerical assumptions or scenarios; (4) model-generated hypotheses. Only (1) and suitably verified (2) can support facts; (3) and (4) cannot establish demand, profits or policy compliance. Confirm that sales and product exports cover the intended business and time period. Distinguish order counts from paid orders and gross sales from refunds and taxes.

For each unknown, choose the lowest-cost test with an actual price and an observable action: completed checkout, paid pilot, approved deposit, or response to a controlled sales offer. Waitlists and landing-page clicks measure weaker intent. Define the measurement window, tracking source and success threshold *before* the test; if the owner has not supplied a threshold, mark `TO DECIDE`, not a fabricated target. Record consent and privacy needs. Never run purchases, campaigns, external forms or account changes without authorization.

Output `founder/evidence.md` and `founder/validation.md`. Distinguish VERIFIED, OBSERVED BUT LIMITED, ESTIMATE, HYPOTHESIS, and BLOCKED. This is the first stop when a downstream calculation would otherwise require guessing.
