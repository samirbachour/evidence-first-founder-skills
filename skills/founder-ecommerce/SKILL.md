# Real-catalog ecommerce decisions

Do **not** infer a product catalog from remembered examples or public snippets. First read an actual product export, live product URLs supplied by the owner, or connected WooCommerce data, scoped to the correct store and date. Identify item/variant IDs, product name, live/visibility status, price, coupon/discount, shipping, fulfillment cost and any related ad SKU attribution. For historical results use order/refund exports covering the chosen window; remove cancelled/unpaid orders when measuring realized revenue and state the handling of taxes/shipping.

Deliver whichever the owner requested: (a) SKU-level contribution and stock/fulfillment exposure; (b) product/category organization from **only actual SKUs**; (c) landing-page friction supported by observed page data; (d) ad profitability with acquisition cost and refunds reconciled; (e) price and bundle scenario based on the `founder-cfo` model. No assumptions about quantities, sales or profitable products from images alone.

Preserve each store's established brand voice, customer claims, and verified product specifications. Distinguish customer-facing benefits from backend operations without omitting legally required information. Do not invent supplier certifications, product materials, discounts or performance results.

Output `founder/ecommerce-audit.md`, a grounded SKU table with sources and dated observations, prioritized fixes and unresolved data questions. Do not publish product changes without approval.
