# Launch through controlled evidence

Read the actual product/service status and `founder/validation.md` before proposing a campaign or sales event. Choose the lowest-cost real-action test appropriate to the business. For an existing store, optimize a clearly identifiable product/landing page and measure completed checkouts, refunds, revenue, attributed cost and support tickets. For services, test paid pilot engagements at current published terms. For unfinished apps, test a genuine user workflow before making adoption projections.

Record: exact offer/product URL, audience/source, owner-approved spend cap, start/end dates, measurement source, decision threshold defined in advance, possible confounders, operational and payment safeguards. If owner approval or source data is missing, write the required fields as `OPEN`, not arbitrary numbers. Do not place live orders, change ads, publish pages or modify policies without authorization.

Give an actionable checklist with critical dependencies: product readiness; payments and refunds; fulfillment or service capacity; legal and advertising review; analytics with actual event verification; customer support; rollback criteria. The first 30-day scorecard should report actual results where available, with missing cells explicit.

Output `founder/launch.md`; send what worked (actual measurements) to `founder-validation`, `founder-marketing`, `founder-offer` and `founder-cfo`.
