# Brand audit and design brief

Use `founder/idea.md`, current owned logo/brand assets, actual site pages, verified competitor positioning, and existing style instructions. Begin with an audit of consistency, recognizability, accessibility and buyer journey. Preserve existing brands unless the owner explicitly requests naming or rebranding.

For a new brand only, propose candidates labeled PROPOSALS, then perform fresh checks: USPTO trademark search (U.S.), relevant foreign official registers, domain registry, and live social handles, with dates, query variants and market/class caveats. No availability or registration claims from memory. Include a warning that search is preliminary, not legal clearance.

If visual concepts are requested, specify the needed format and use the appropriate design/image tooling. Do not generate mockups when the user asks only for analysis. For contrast, verify text/background pairs against the applicable Web Content Accessibility Guidelines standard, with linked contrast results rather than guessing a ratio.

Never invent a client's brand voice or claim customer results without observed evidence. Output `founder/brand.md`, keeping sourced current elements separate from proposed changes.
