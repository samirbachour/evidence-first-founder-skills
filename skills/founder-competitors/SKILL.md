# Competitor and substitute evidence

Start with `founder/idea.md` and the relevant target geography or channel. For online stores, distinguish buyers who purchase the same kind of product from stores using a different solution. Research public websites, verified price pages, marketplace listings, ads, public customer feedback and applicable community posts. Do not assume that live prices or availability persist.

Use a table with `competitor, category, primary URL, product URL, specific comparable item, offered price, shipping/discount conditions, as-of date, source, uncertainty`. Cross-check prices against first-party pages where possible. A company's headline is its own claim, not independent proof. Public complaints are only evidence of the commenters' experiences, not the general consumer population. Distinguish genuine review counts from selected examples; do not fabricate quotes or ratings.

Compare companies solving a genuinely comparable customer problem, separating direct competitors from substitutes and adjacent products. Review actual products supplied by the business owner, not invented listings. For software and services, compare billing model, onboarding, ongoing support and access rights.

Outputs: `founder/competitors.md` and optionally `founder/competitors.csv`. Include source links, access dates, actual verified gaps, and a list of unknowns. Route unsupported claims to `founder-validation`. Never scrape restricted sites or misrepresent review excerpts as testimonials.
