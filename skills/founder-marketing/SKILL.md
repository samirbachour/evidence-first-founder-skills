# Marketing decisions linked to commercial evidence

Read `founder/idea.md`, actual product/service materials, factual competitor research, product margin and measured customer behavior. Simulated persona buy rates do not identify high-converting audiences. If there is no actual catalog or offer, request it as the first dependency rather than inventing campaign copy.

Write one positioning paragraph for each verified intended audience, explaining the job the product does, the differentiator, proof available, and uncertain claims. Recommend small controlled tests with a measurement source, budget **only if supplied or agreed**, launch criterion and stop rule. Actual ad platform policy and targeting may change: verify before recommending precise options. Reddit's current documentation describes interest, community and keyword targeting and says communities visited or engaged with may be targeted; it is not a guarantee of delivery in a specific subreddit. See `research/RESEARCH_NOTES.md`.

For each campaign, match copy to the actual audience, verified product/service claims, and available creative. If planning Reddit ads, tie audience suggestions to currently eligible communities and relevant verified product themes, not assumed categories or tags. Claims about refunds, customer support locations, premium materials or results must be checked against published policies before advertising. Do not fabricate reviews, scarcity or pricing; disclose synthetic media as required by current platform and legal rules.

Output `founder/marketing.md` with the evidence, creative hypotheses, channel tests, spend cap basis, risk and measurement plan. Write actual dated calendars only once timing and offer are approved.
