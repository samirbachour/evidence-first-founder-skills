# Operations and delivery audit

Read verified current store/service workflows and live integrations or owner documentation. For an online made-to-order apparel business, document: item/variant identity, order received, manufacturing routing, tracking, fulfilment communication, refund/exchange handling, cancellations, failed-payment handling, support and retained records. Separate user-facing brand language from back-end supplier operations.

For a consulting service, verify booking, preparation, payment/refund commitments, support hours and deliverables. For software products, verify actual deployment state, monitoring, authentication, privacy, backups, incident response and service support. Do not confuse a product idea with a deployed feature.

Gather current supplier costs, processing terms, payout timing, regional taxes and relevant consumer rules from dated sources. Any unavailable pricing is `QUOTE REQUIRED`; do not invent a supplier or quote. For U.S. sales, confirm current Federal Trade Commission rules on endorsements, reviews and advertising and state requirements with official sources. Mark legal, health, trademark and safety matters for specialist verification rather than issuing legal clearance.

Write `founder/ops.md`: exact workflow, bottlenecks, people/hours/costs with sources, incident handling, dated compliance checklist, and risk register. Route verified cost corrections to `founder-cfo`.
