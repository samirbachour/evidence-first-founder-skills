# Online store input checklist (no values supplied)

Use the same store and date window for each export. Remove customer names, addresses, emails, payment details and other personal information before sharing where practical.

- [ ] Current product export with product/variation IDs, names, visibility, item type, price, sale price and category.
- [ ] Order line items with date, payment status, product/variation IDs, quantity, unit and discount amounts, currency, refunds, shipping charged, tax and totals.
- [ ] Fulfilment/provider charges keyed to order or SKU and actual costs/fees, including retailer-funded shipping.
- [ ] Payment processor charges: flat fee, percentage, payout timing and any chargeback amounts.
- [ ] Advertising spend and attributed transactions (with channel, campaign and date windows).
- [ ] Real refund/return rate with policy and direct handling cost.
- [ ] Recurring store expenses, returns/replacements, owner labor and support cost.
- [ ] Current published shipping, refund and service policies.
- [ ] Explicit 12-month scenario entered *only after* baseline costs and real historical demand have been reviewed.

If one of these is unavailable, record the gap and exclude unsupported metrics from the decision.
