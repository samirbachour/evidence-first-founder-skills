# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — 2026-11-19

Independent MIT-licensed adaptation of [Jake Schincariol's founder-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/founder-skill).

- Added `founder-validation`, `founder-ecommerce` and `founder-prioritize` to the original 11 skills.
- Corrected financial planning: scenario-based verdicts, required cost declarations, monthly order forecasts, processing-fee and refund assumptions, caveats about omitted costs and cash timing.
- Removed any use of synthetic customer buy rates as evidence of market demand or a business-plan verdict.
- Labeled simulated customers as fictional objection exercises, not real market research.
- Separated human pricing surveys from synthetic or unverified responses.
- Made skill instructions portable across ChatGPT, Codex and other compatible agents; included a `plugin.json` manifest without claiming marketplace publication.
- Added an evidence ledger, blank templates, research bibliography and offline regression tests.
- Released a generic edition with no business-specific playbooks or private account data.

See [`research/RESEARCH_NOTES.md`](research/RESEARCH_NOTES.md) for sources and methodological limits. Original MIT license and copyright remain in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
