## Major methodological corrections

1. **Synthetic customer panels are not customers.** A review in *Psychology & Marketing* found mixed outcomes when language models are used as synthetic research participants, with greatest value in upstream pretesting: [Wiley, 2024](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/mar.21982). Another study found that synthetic samples often underestimate heterogeneity and cannot support ordinary statistical inference: [Cambridge University Press, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1017/pan.2024.5).
2. **Newer work cautions against using synthetic price responses.** A September 28, 2026 study explicitly compared synthetic willingness-to-pay and incentive-compatible real responses across four consumer product studies. It found economically plausible artificial responses that nonetheless implied materially different price/profit decisions: [Oetzel & Maiberger, *Journal of Marketing Analytics*, 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/s41270-026-00542-7). Do not treat the upstream `100 simulated buyers` as a conversion survey.
3. **Synthetic propensity can overpredict purchase conversion.** Research benchmarking large language model survey responses against human brand tracking found funnel-stage distortions, including overprediction of consideration and purchase: [2025 paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5145114). A 2026 review likewise describes inflated ratings and reduced variance: [NIM Marketing Intelligence Review, 2026](https://reference-global.com/article/10.2478/nimmir-2026-0008?tab=article).
4. **Financial calculators are scenario engines.** Verified prices and costs do not validate the volume ramp. Inventory cash timing, sales tax, actual refunds, discounts, payment fees and marketing costs require separate coverage. The revised Python script validates inputs and displays model limitations.

- Ordinary ChatGPT conversations do not automatically install plugins from a ZIP. Not all modes can execute bundled scripts. Use Projects for a simple user-accessible start, Work/plugin packaging where available, and Codex/local Python for deterministic tools.

## Marketing policy and contemporary platform documentation

- [Federal Trade Commission: rule on consumer reviews and testimonials](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials): the rule took effect October 21, 2024. It prohibits specified fake consumer reviews and testimonials, including false AI-generated consumer experiences. The toolkit bans synthetic panel quotes in testimonials and forbids false scarcity, performance and refund claims.
- [Reddit Business: audience targeting](https://business.reddithelp.com/articles/Knowledge/reddit-audiences): interest, community and keyword audiences. Community targeting may reach users active in communities beyond the selected subreddit page; some communities are ineligible. Targeting configuration and community availability should be checked during campaign setup, rather than hardcoded into the toolkit.
- [Reddit: advertising policy overview](https://business.reddithelp.com/articles/Knowledge/Reddit-Advertising-Policy-Overview): includes deceptive claims, dating services, user data, intellectual property and other review areas. A given advertiser must check its own campaign against the current policy and platform classification rules. Do not assume a brand or audience is approved for a given ad category.
- [United States Patent and Trademark Office: search](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search) and [W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/) are the authoritative starting points for name-clearance and accessibility checks; they are not substitutes for counsel or testing.
